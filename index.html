OUR STORY

Every harvest begins with a question.

Ours took nine years, a lot of soil and a few stubborn people to answer.

2017

It began with a question.

Why does the soil give less every year — when farmers give it more?

The search begins.
2018

Back to the soil.

Natural inputs, tested by hand in the fields. The first seed of an idea.

The first experiments.
2019–20

The quiet years.

Season after season of field trials — learning what tired soil really needs.

Patience, not shortcuts.
2021

From the lab to the land.

The first formulation leaves the bench and enters real farms — real crops, real seasons, real results.

The first harvests on living soil.
2023

The soil spoke. Science listened.

The living biology inside our product is identified at CSIR-NCL, Pune.

Proof, not promises.
2025

The seed becomes a company.

NectarGuard Biotech is born — a production campus, a growing team and a promise to every soil we touch.

From an idea to an institution.
2026

Today, the soil smiles back.

From terraces to vineyards, growers across Maharashtra are growing with living soil.
